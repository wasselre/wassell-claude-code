## What it is (in plain English)
The ability reads what a customer said about *where* they want to buy — in their WhatsApp chats and in transcribed phone calls — and turns each place they mention into a structured "evidence" record: which place, whether they want it or reject it, how strongly, and whose preference it is. Those records feed a review-first proposal that a sales rep confirms before anything reaches the client's profile. An admin grades the AI's readings one card at a time on `/geo-grade` (Right / Wrong / Not sure), with the exact transcript the AI read shown on the card.

## Why it exists
Reps type location preferences inconsistently or not at all. The Project Finder needs a clean, boundary-verified geography preference per client. The ability extracts it from the conversations that already exist, but never trusts itself: every proposal is human-confirmed, and the grader measures how often the AI's reading is right before the gate is ever opened.

## Key behaviors
- **One conversation = one channel.** The backfill gathers a client's history as separate conversations: one per `phone_calls` transcript (`channel='call'`, id = the phone_calls record id) and one per WhatsApp thread (`channel='chat'`, id = the `chat_wid`). They are never merged. Each is extracted, persisted and reviewed on its own, so a client with one call and one chat gets **two** pending proposals — a chat review is separate from a call review (operator decision, 2026-09-13).
- **Calls use Hatif's diarized transcript (added 2026-09-13, after grading).** `phone_calls.transcription_text` is Hatif's flattened one-line text with no speakers, but `call_logs.transcription.words[]` (same id as the call record) is diarized for every transcribed call — each word carries a `speaker` (`ch_0/ch_1` audio channels, or `speaker_N`); Hatif's resolved `role` (Agent/Customer) exists on only ~3.6%. `hatifDialogue.ts` builds speaker-labelled turns and decides who the agent is in this order: Hatif `role` → the ONE speaker who introduces the company («معك فهد من وصل العقارية», strict regex) → channel scheme on an outbound call (`ch_0` = our leg, measured) → otherwise turns are kept but every speaker is `unknown`. The result is recorded as `Conversation.speaker_labels`, and the prompt switches between `CALL_LABELLED_RULES` (trust the labels; an agent-offered place is still not a client preference) and the unlabelled `CALL_TRANSCRIPT_RULES` (infer the speaker from content; default `speaker='unknown'`). Each turn's `source_timestamp` is call_time plus the first word's offset. Chat messages carry `flow` (in/out), so the speaker is a fact. **Never assume Retell** — calls are Hatif; Retell was a 9-call AI-agent experiment in July 2026.
- **Per-mention provenance.** Every evidence row stores `source_channel`, `source_ref` and `source_timestamp` from the specific turn it came from: the model's `turn` index when that turn actually contains the span, else the unique turn containing the span verbatim, else the conversation's first turn. For a call the ref is the call record itself. Extractor version `geo-extract/v8`.
- **Agent-only chat threads are skipped** (the customer never wrote) — nothing to interpret, no LLM spend.
- **Not every word is a place, not every place a preference (2026-09-13 grading fixes).** Generic words («الموقع», «المكان», a «يا الشيخ» address) are not location mentions; a place named only for comparison or as the customer's origin («في الخبر أرخص») is `preference_role='none'`; a relative reference that restates already-recorded places («بين المناطق هذي») gets no separate record.
- **A side of a ROAD is a band, not a city zone (2026-09-15).** «غرب الملك فهد» is one `direction` anchor. The resolver reads the direction word, then tries the referent as a CITY (zone of its districts), then as a ROAD (a bounded `directional_band` on that side, default depth), and otherwise `needs_confirm('side_of_unknown_referent')`. Riyadh has both a King Fahd Road and a King Fahd District; only a line has sides, so a district is never guessed as the referent of a side. A mention with a district AND a side («العليا (غرب الملك فهد)») compiles to TWO include clauses of the group (AND): the band, plus the district — never a district id inside the band's road list. Element search passes the city in ENGLISH (the RPC filters `geo_elements.city='Riyadh'`) and also queries the «طريق …» form so the road outranks hospitals/metro stops that share the name.
- **Districts clipped to a side of a road = a custom shape (2026-09-15, second pass).** The operator's read of the first calib-003 map: «he wanted those districts but west of the road, not the entire length of the road». So a road side stated with districts is a QUALIFIER, not a separate area: `distributeRoadSide` (orchestrator) turns every include district ref in the expression into `district_side_clip` (district ids + the road, `side`) and removes the standalone band — including across groups, because the compiler puts each independent mention in its own OR group. At proposal time `hydrateClipGeometry` calls `wassell_districts_side_of_road(uuid[], road, side)` (PostGIS: split each district by the road, keep the pieces whose centroid is on that side; a district entirely on the other side is dropped and reported) and stores `clip_geojson` + per-district `clip_parts` NEXT TO the recipe (never instead of it). The review apply path turns the shape into one `drawn_area` location item per polygon (closed ring, `[lng, lat]`), falling back to districts + a side rule when no shape exists; the grader draws the shape and the card reads «حدّد: العليا (3.89 من 10.84 كم²)، المحمدية … — غرب طريق الملك فهد». Excludes are never distributed; two different road sides in one expression are left alone; diagonal sides are refused by the RPC.
- **Spelling and article tolerance (2026-09-15).** Candidate lookup queries lexical variants (ة/ه, ى/ي, أإآ/ا, with/without «ال»), and the selection key is article-insensitive — «نرجس» ≡ «حي النرجس», «المحمديه» ≡ «حي المحمدية» — while still exact (الجبيلة ≠ الجبيل). A mangled `normalized_token` falls back to the verbatim span.
- **A direction + its city is ONE zone (2026-09-15, third pass — written by Kimi from a Claude spec).** «شمال الرياض» is extracted as two anchors (direction + city); the direction resolves to the city's northern districts and the city anchor to the CITY record itself. The merge now drops city-anchor recipes when a `zone_union` is present (the zone already IS that scope), so the proposal carries only district ids — the city uuid no longer rides along as if it were a district. The grader also reads the WHOLE chat: transcripts are paged (1,000 rows a page, never a cross-chat cap — the old single `limit(600)` silently hid conversations whose messages sorted late) and shown up to 40,000 characters; a zone or any list of more than six districts is summarised as «حدّد: شمال الرياض — 34 حيًا» instead of listing every name. A zone's ids are district record ids, so the review conversion now emits DISTRICT items for a zone (it used to emit inside_area element rules, which the SQL compiler cannot resolve from a district id — the zone drew nothing on the map); the grader's chip row collapses a zone into one chip with the district count.
- **Curated city zones (Settings → City Zones, 2026-09-21).** What «شمال الرياض» means is DATA per (city, zone), not a rule: `geo_zone_overrides` rows win, else the coordinate default (each district's centroid position inside the city's bounding box). An admin edits the set on a map at `/settings/geo-zones` — click districts, or search them by name — and the resolver, the Project Finder and `src/lib/market/zones.ts` all keep reading the SAME `wassell_city_zone_districts(city, zone)` RPC, so a save takes effect everywhere at once. Saving an EMPTY set clears the curation and hands the zone back to the coordinate default, which is what the ~186 cities nobody curates use. The operator's reason (2026-09-21): “what is known as the north district of Riyadh is different from the districts you selected, which are in the north of Riyadh on paper.” Riyadh’s `north` is the one curated zone today — 34 districts, against 29 the coordinates alone would pick.
- **Our project names are not places; English-letter district names resolve (2026-09-27).** Grading calib-003 showed 6 of 8 wrong mentions were OUR projects read as map anchors («مينا 52» → pin, «الشقه هذي» → «الماجدية 163»), plus «Malga» left unresolved. The backfill port now loads every all_projects name (paged, never capped), reduces each to its head («مينا 52 - النرجس» → «مينا 52»), drops any head that is also a district name («الفلاح» stays a district), and hands the list to the extractor: the prompt names the heads that occur in the conversation as projects-not-places, and the parser drops any anchor whose span is one of them (a mention left without anchors is dropped with its relations). Single-word heads must equal the whole span; only numbered or multi-word heads match as substrings. Separately, a Latin span («Malga», «Yasmeen») now reaches the district's English name («Al Malqa Dist.») through transliteration spelling variants at the candidate stage and an exact transliteration key (article stripped, q/g/k, ee/i, oo/u folded) at the selection gate — still exact, never fuzzy. Files: `projectGuard.ts`, `latinNames.ts`.
- **The VERIFIER — a second AI checks the map against the conversation (2026-09-27, advisory only).** After the backfill writes a pending proposal, a second LLM call (`verifier.ts`, `geo-verify/v1`) re-reads the SAME conversation (numbered exactly as the extractor saw it, call rules and project-name block included) next to every extracted mention rendered as one Arabic sentence of what the AI put on the map (`placementText.ts` — the same wording as the grader's placement line; mentions the system left off the map are listed too, as «not a preference — nothing on the map»). Per mention it answers: is this really a place, really the CUSTOMER's preference (not the salesperson's suggestion, a comparison, a project name, a word like «عظم»), the right polarity, the right place — verdict `right | wrong_place | not_a_preference | not_a_place | wrong_polarity | unsure` with a one-line reason — plus places the customer clearly wanted that are MISSING. `overall` is `doubt` if any mention is not `right` or anything is missing, else `agree`. Stored on the proposal (`geo_pref_proposals.verifier`, `verifier_version`, `verified_at`); it NEVER changes the proposal, the gate or a client record. Routing = the extractor's (stub → DeepSeek → Claude Haiku, metered). If every provider fails the stored result is `status:'error', overall:'unknown'` — never "agree"; the job does not fail over it, but a failure to WRITE the result does. The conversation grader shows a pill next to the conversation header (✓ «المراجع يوافق» / ⚠ «المراجع يشكّ» / «المراجع لم يعمل»), a line under each mention (✓ agrees / ⚠ doubts: kind + reason) and «قد يكون فاته: …» above the map. **Measured on calib-002 (107 human-graded mentions: 105 right, 2 wrong; 26 conversations; run on Claude Haiku because the worktree had no DeepSeek key):** first pass agreed on 47 of the 105 "right" mentions — 21 of its complaints were a verdict-meaning mix-up (it answered «not a preference» for mentions the system had ALSO left off as not-a-preference), so the parser now maps «not a preference / not a place» on an off-map mention to agreement and the prompt says so. Second pass: agrees on **71 of 105** "right" mentions (68%) and flags **1 of the 2** "wrong" ones; 0 provider errors; 17 "missed" places reported. Not all 34 remaining alarms are the verifier's mistakes — at least 3 are real map errors the per-mention grade does not see: «الحمرة» placed in أم القيوين (UAE) for a Riyadh customer, and two refusals («… هذي كلها ما أبيها», «ما أبي جهة الروضة») whose reading is `negative` but whose clause on the map is an INCLUDE. As it stands the verifier is too suspicious to gate anything on; it is a reviewer's second pair of eyes.
- **The card reads by itself, and rep decisions are the accuracy meter (2026-09-27).** `shouldAutoRead` (`src/lib/geo/geoCardAutoRead.ts`, simplified the same day by the chat auto-read): the card reads on open (trigger `open`) iff the customer has written something unread (`prefs.unread_customer_messages > 0`, counted against the `chat_read_state` watermarks); never on graded evidence or inside the 60 s cool-down. The old «≥3 messages» / «once an hour» rules are gone — the server's keyword gate and lease replace them. `v_geo_pref_review_outcomes` (migration `2026-09-27_02`, security_invoker) gives per Riyadh day: saved unchanged / saved after edit / dismissed / deferred and `unchanged_rate` — the share of readings a rep saved without touching, which is the live accuracy measure.
- **A place only the salesperson said is never drawn (2026-09-27, found by the verifier on a live card).** The rep's unanswered «فيه فلل بالصحافة والنرجس» was extracted with `speaker='agent'` but `preference_role='positive'`, and nothing downstream checked the speaker, so the card showed both districts as «يريد». `companyRules.ts` RULE 2 demotes any agent-spoken positive/negative mention to `preference_role='none'` before compile (kept for the record, never on the map). A suggestion the customer ACCEPTS is extracted from the customer's own reply, so it is unaffected.
- **A customer's question about a place is interest (operator rule, 2026-09-27).** `companyRules.ts` runs at the start of `runReviewFirst`: a CLIENT-spoken `question` the extractor read as `exploratory` (for the customer's own purchase, not counterfactual) is promoted to a soft active positive (`commitment` ≥ acceptable, `modality='inferred'`), so «فيه الازدهار موجود؟» / «حي الفاروق؟» reach the map. The stored evidence is untouched — a re-review applies it. The extractor prompt and the verifier prompt (`geo-verify/v2`) state the same rule. On calib-002 it moved 10 customer questions onto the map. Verifier agreement with the human grades stayed ~65/105 across runs (64–71 on Claude Haiku) — run-to-run noise is as large as any effect, so the verifier is a highlighter, not a gate.
- **Refusals listed together are excluded; no district is picked abroad for an anchored client (2026-09-27, found by the verifier).** An `any_of` whose members include refusals («ميكفاي، الأندلس، هذي كلها ما أبيها») used to compile as ONE include clause — the refused places were drawn as wanted. Now each negative member of an `any_of` is its own exclude clause and only the positive members share the include. Separately, when a district name has no same-country match, the resolver no longer falls back to a namesake abroad if the client has an established city: «الحمرة» from a Riyadh customer had resolved to الحمرة in أم القيوين; it now asks (needs_confirm 'outside_admin').
- **A target-less `exception` never inverts a member (2026-09-15).** The extractor sometimes emits `exception` with no target for "not east, but west"; the compiler used to turn EVERY member into an exclude, which recorded a customer's hard requirement («غرب الملك فهد») as an exclusion. Members now keep their own polarity and `needs_confirm` is still raised.
- **Grammar never decides meaning.** A question («عندكم فلل بالنرجس؟») is still a positive preference; a conditional («إذا مو شمال الرياض ما يناسبني») is still active and hard.
- **The rep path: a confirm card in every client-linked WhatsApp chat (2026-09-27).** `GeoPrefCard` (mounted in `ChatDetail` next to the study card; hidden when the chat header is collapsed) asks `GET /api/geo-preference/chat-card?clientId&chatWid` for the card of THAT conversation (the chat wid is the conversation id). States: never read → «لم تُقرأ هذه المحادثة بعد» + «اقرأ المواقع»; read with nothing placed → «لم يذكر العميل أماكن بعد»; an open proposal → «يريد» / «لا يريد» groups, one row per mention with the grader's own wording (`placementLine`, moved to `src/pages/GeoGrade/lib/placementLine.ts` so rep and grader read the same sentence), ticked by default, the verifier's doubt as an amber line under it and its «missed» places as one muted line, plus an optional map of the ticked lines; saved → the saved lines with «✓ حُفظت في تفضيلات العميل»; dismissed / replaced → «تم تجاهل القراءة». «رسائل جديدة منذ آخر قراءة» appears when the CUSTOMER wrote after the reading (agent messages do not count — only the customer's words can change what they want). **Save** posts to `/api/geo-preference/review`: `confirm` when every line is ticked, else `edit` with the expression pruned of the unticked mentions (`pruneGeoExpression`: drop every `geo:<id>` ref, then emptied clauses, then emptied groups), always with `expectedVersion`; a 409 (someone else resolved it) reloads the card. **Unresolved lines (bare names, no real place) are shown but can never be ticked and are always pruned** — measured on a live proposal, a plain `confirm` would have written three element rules whose element id is an Arabic word («المعذر», «الشمالي», «طريق الملك خالد»). **Dismiss** = `reject`. The card never writes a client record itself.
- **Reading one chat on demand (`POST /api/geo-preference/chat-card {action:'analyze'}`, 2026-09-27).** Any rep who can see the client (RLS check with the rep's own token) may run it; the pipeline runs on the service client and writes only what the backfill writes. Mode (`decideAnalyzeMode`): a full **extraction** when the chat was never read, or the customer wrote since and none of its evidence is graded; otherwise a **review-only rerun** that rebuilds evidence + relations from the stored rows (no extraction LLM call). **Graded data is never re-extracted** — a checkpoint or evidence id that has a `geo_pref_labels` row or sits in any calibration batch's `subjects` forces the rerun, because extraction deletes and re-mints those rows. Before the new proposal is minted (rerun) or right after it (extraction — so a failed extraction leaves the rep's old proposal alone), this conversation's older `pending` proposals become `superseded` (`superseded_by` = the new one); non-pending proposals are never touched. The verifier then runs on the new proposal (its failure is stored, not thrown). A conversation read in the last 60 seconds is not read again (`mode:'skipped_recent'`). First live run (2026-09-27, one real chat, 9 mentions): extraction + verifier in 33 s.
- **Chat auto-read — the per-minute cron + open-chat trigger (2026-09-27).** Live traffic that day: ~46 customer WhatsApp messages/day over ~15 chats, median 20 characters, 71% of a customer's consecutive messages < 3 min apart (median 42 s), reps reply after a median 95 s — so there is NO AI classifier; every read sees the whole conversation. `chat_read_candidates` (SQL, service-role) lists the (chat, client) pairs — chat → client through the `chats` record's `wid` + `client_link` (scalar or array), group chats excluded — with unread inbound text-ish messages (a body, or a voice note's transcript) after the lower of the two watermarks, or a voice note still being transcribed (< 10 min old). `selectDueChats` (pure, `api/_lib/clientPrefs/dueSelection.ts`): leased → skip; failures back off 2, 4 … 60 min and stop after 8 in a row (left to the rep); otherwise wait until 90 s after the newest unread message, and while a transcript is pending, unless the oldest unread is ≥ 10 min old; then the keyword gate (`keywordGate.ts`: any digit, any stem from `GATE_STEMS` word-ishly matched — «حي» never inside «صحيح» — or ≥ 25 normalised characters; pure pleasantries/emoji never pass). A gated batch advances BOTH watermarks (`last_outcome='gate_skipped'`) with no model call. `readChatForClient` (`readChat.ts`) is the ONE read for cron / open / manual: verify the chat is linked to the client, compute the unread batch, gate (cron/open), claim the lease (300 s; one winner), capture the watermark (newest unread), gather the conversation ONCE, run `analyzeChatConversation` (this pipeline, unchanged, handed the gathered conversation) and the preference agent (`extractChatPrefs`) with `Promise.allSettled`, then `chat_read_finish`. Each agent runs only when its own watermark is behind (manual runs both), so a partial failure retries only the failed agent; a geo `skipped_recent` does not advance the geo watermark; a failure never advances; the lease is released whenever finish is not reached. `auto_write_enabled` stays false — both agents only mint proposals.
- **Transcribed voice notes are customer text (2026-09-27).** An inbound `kind='audio'` message with a `chat_messages.transcript` (filled by the inbound-media worker) is rendered as the customer line «(رسالة صوتية) <transcript>» in the gathered conversation, counts toward `customer_messages` / staleness, and toward "unread". A voice note with no usable transcript (status none/failed, or media lost) shows as an amber mic badge on the card instead.
- **Review-first, always.** The orchestrator's only side effect is one `pending` row in `geo_pref_proposals` per conversation; `auto_write_enabled` is forced off in the backfill's run context regardless of the DB flag.
- **Idempotent per conversation.** Re-running a client replaces the prior model rows for each of its conversations (by real conversation id); evidence ids are re-minted, so a calibration batch built on old ids must be rebuilt.
- **Grader is admin-only** and shows, per card, the channel («مكالمة هاتفية» / «محادثة واتساب»), the customer's first name, the ONE transcript the mention came from with the span highlighted, the phrase, and the AI's plain-Arabic reading. Keyboard 1/2/3.
- **Conversation grader (`?view=chat`, added 2026-09-13).** One screen per conversation: the chat as bubbles (or the call transcript), every mention with its reading and a verdict, then a **map of what the AI actually selected** — the proposal's location items drawn from the real district boundaries (`wassell_district_shapes_by_ids`) and compiled element rules (`wassell_preview_geo_items`); include = copper, exclude = red — with a map verdict per conversation saved as a `geo_pref_labels` row (`subject_kind='checkpoint'`, `field='map.verdict'`). The map reads like a normal map: the Wassel basemap with Google's district and road names ON (the picker's label-suppressed style is NOT used here), a name pill on every drawn shape (colliding pills nudged apart), the view fitted to the WANTED shapes only (an exclude band along a 40 km road must not zoom the map out), excludes drawn light and underneath. Each mention also says what it became on the map («حدّد: النرجس (الرياض)», «لم يُحدَّد حي حقيقي — يحتاج تأكيدًا», or «ليس تفضيلًا»).
- **The map is real now (fixed 2026-09-13).** Until then the compiler wrote the district *names* into the expression (`geo_data_version='stub'`) and the resolver's real output only fed the gate signals — every proposal carried names, never district ids, so nothing was ever selected on the map. `mergeResolutionsIntoPreference` (orchestrator) now overwrites each mention's stub recipe with the resolver recipe (district / element ids) when every anchor of that mention resolved; a partially resolved mention keeps the stub so `geo_data_version !== 'stub'` always means real ids. Two prerequisites landed with it: the runner reviews on the **persisted** evidence ids (so `geo:<id>` refs and `source_evidence_ids` join to `geo_pref_evidence`), and the resolver gets an **established city** — the client's single `preferred_city`, else Riyadh as the organizational default — because bare names are namesakes across the Kingdom (النرجس ×6 cities, الياسمين ×20) and the resolver rightly refuses to guess without a city.

## User flows
1. **Backfill (admin):** `POST /api/geo-preference/backfill {action:'enqueue', clientIds}` → `{action:'process'}` in bounded batches → per client: gather conversations → per conversation: extract → persist evidence + one checkpoint → review-first → pending proposal. Progress via `GET ?runId`.
2. **Grade (admin):** open `/geo-grade?batch=<id>` → cards ordered by client then time → Right / Wrong / Not sure saves one `overall.verdict` label per evidence row → "All done" screen; verdicts are editable by re-visiting a card.
3. **Review (rep):** open proposals listed by `/api/geo-preference/proposals`; confirm / reject / edit via `/api/geo-preference/review` (audit row per action). A confirm/edit IS the apply — the human tap is the authorization; `auto_write_enabled` only governs the (off) automatic path.
4. **Confirm in the chat (rep, 2026-09-27):** open a client-linked WhatsApp chat → the «تفضيلات العميل» card (usually already read by the cron, or read on open when the customer wrote something unread; «اقرأ المواقع» / «أعد القراءة» force a read, up to a minute) → untick any wrong line → «احفظ في ملف العميل» (writes the ticked lines to the client's `location_items`, merged, never wiping what is there) or «تجاهل». Later, «حدّث» re-reads the chat after the customer writes again.
5. **Empty states:** a client with no calls and no client-written chat completes with no proposal; a card whose transcript is missing shows «لا يوجد نص محادثة محفوظ لهذا العميل».

## Data touched
- Reads: `unified_records` (models `phone_calls` → `transcription_text`, `call_time`, `client_link`; `chats` → `wid`, `client_link`), `chat_messages` (`id`, `flow`, `kind`, `body`, `transcript`, `transcript_status`, `media_saved`, `date`), `geo_pref_gate_config`, `geo_pref_calibration_batch.subjects`, `chat_read_state`.
- Chat auto-read writes (service role, through RPCs only): `chat_read_state` (watermarks, last outcome, failure count, lease — `chat_read_claim` / `chat_read_finish` / `chat_read_mark_gate_skipped` / `chat_read_release`).
- Writes (service role, never a client record): `geo_pref_evidence` (`conversation_id` = phone_calls id or chat_wid, `source_channel`, `source_ref`, `source_timestamp`), `geo_pref_relations`, `geo_pref_checkpoints` (one per conversation, `turn_id='aggregate'`, `as_of_timestamp` = the conversation's last turn, `member_message_ids` = turn refs), `geo_pref_proposals` (one per conversation; the verifier writes ONLY its `verifier` / `verifier_version` / `verified_at` columns), `geo_pref_labels` (grader verdicts), `geo_pref_backfill_jobs`. The chat card's analyze writes the same tables as the backfill plus `geo_pref_proposals.status='superseded'` / `superseded_by` on this conversation's older pending proposals.
- The ONLY client-record write: `records.data.location_items` of the client, via `/api/geo-preference/review` (confirm / edit), merged onto what is there through `record_save`, with one `geo_pref_review_audit` row per action.

## Open questions / known limitations
- **Re-backfill of the 23 `calib-001` clients has NOT been run yet.** Old rows (`conversation_id` = client uuid) must be purged first and the batch rebuilt — runbook §2a.
- **Calibration grading 2026-09-13 (batch `calib-001` after the per-channel re-extraction, one grader):** 90 right / 13 wrong / 0 unsure (87%; calls 83/94, chats 7/9). 6 of the 13 were speaker attribution on unlabelled calls (the rep's scripted opener «لك رغبة بشراء شقة أو دور بشمال الرياض» read as the customer's wish; القيروان; the reverse once) — that is what the Hatif-diarization change above targets; 3 were non-place words, 1 a comparison city, 1 a redundant relative reference, 2 (`التعاون`/`الازدهار`, a customer question) are still under discussion. The diarized path has NOT been re-graded yet.
- Inbound calls have no channel rule (too few channel-labelled inbound calls on prod to measure), so an inbound call whose agent never introduces the company keeps `speaker='unknown'` turns.
- A short span that appears in several turns («الرياض») with no usable `turn` hint falls back to conversation-level provenance (first turn's ref).
- Re-running a client re-mints its checkpoint, and the old pending proposal's `checkpoint_id` is nulled by the FK — the proposal-store dedup then cannot match it, so purge or supersede old pending proposals before a re-run. (The chat card's analyze does this itself.)
- **A plain `confirm` through `/api/geo-preference/review` writes unresolved lines as junk (found 2026-09-27, NOT fixed there).** `geoPreferenceToLocationItems` turns a `geo_data_version='stub'` recipe — whose ids are bare names — into location items whose element id is an Arabic word; the grader hides only non-uuid DISTRICT items. The chat card is safe (it always prunes unresolved lines and sends `edit`); any other surface that confirms a proposal as-is is not.
- **A chat is read from its NEWEST 120 messages** (`MAX_MESSAGES_PER_CHAT`; fixed 2026-09-27 — until then it read the OLDEST 120, so a long chat's latest messages were never seen). What the customer said before those 120 is not re-read.
- **Graded conversations cannot pick up new messages** — the rerun is review-only by design, so the card says so instead of offering «حدّث».
- **Concurrent reads of one chat are serialised by the `chat_read_state` lease (2026-09-27)** — a second reader gets `not_claimed`. A crashed reader's lease expires after 300 s.
- **The chat auto-read's SQL candidate scan only looks back 7 days**, and a chat that failed 8 reads in a row is left to the rep (an open / «أعد القراءة» read that succeeds resets the count). A voice note whose media was lost never flips its `transcript_status` off `pending` (the worker's terminal path does not write it), so it holds a read back for at most 10 minutes.
