## What it is (in plain English)
The ability reads what a customer said about *where* they want to buy — in their WhatsApp chats and in transcribed phone calls — and turns each place they mention into a structured "evidence" record: which place, whether they want it or reject it, how strongly, and whose preference it is. Those records feed a review-first proposal that a sales rep confirms before anything reaches the client's profile. An admin grades the AI's readings one card at a time on `/geo-grade` (Right / Wrong / Not sure), with the exact transcript the AI read shown on the card.

## Why it exists
Reps type location preferences inconsistently or not at all. The Project Finder needs a clean, boundary-verified geography preference per client. The ability extracts it from the conversations that already exist, but never trusts itself: every proposal is human-confirmed, and the grader measures how often the AI's reading is right before the gate is ever opened.

## Key behaviors
- **One conversation = one channel.** The backfill gathers a client's history as separate conversations: one per `phone_calls` transcript (`channel='call'`, id = the phone_calls record id) and one per WhatsApp thread (`channel='chat'`, id = the `chat_wid`). They are never merged. Each is extracted, persisted and reviewed on its own, so a client with one call and one chat gets **two** pending proposals — a chat review is separate from a call review (operator decision, 2026-09-13).
- **Calls use Hatif's diarized transcript (added 2026-09-13, after grading).** `phone_calls.transcription_text` is Hatif's flattened one-line text with no speakers, but `call_logs.transcription.words[]` (same id as the call record) is diarized for every transcribed call — each word carries a `speaker` (`ch_0/ch_1` audio channels, or `speaker_N`); Hatif's resolved `role` (Agent/Customer) exists on only ~3.6%. `hatifDialogue.ts` builds speaker-labelled turns and decides who the agent is in this order: Hatif `role` → the ONE speaker who introduces the company («معك فهد من وصل العقارية», strict regex) → channel scheme on an outbound call (`ch_0` = our leg, measured) → otherwise turns are kept but every speaker is `unknown`. The result is recorded as `Conversation.speaker_labels`, and the prompt switches between `CALL_LABELLED_RULES` (trust the labels; an agent-offered place is still not a client preference) and the unlabelled `CALL_TRANSCRIPT_RULES` (infer the speaker from content; default `speaker='unknown'`). Each turn's `source_timestamp` is call_time plus the first word's offset. Chat messages carry `flow` (in/out), so the speaker is a fact. **Never assume Retell** — calls are Hatif; Retell was a 9-call AI-agent experiment in July 2026.
- **Per-mention provenance.** Every evidence row stores `source_channel`, `source_ref` and `source_timestamp` from the specific turn it came from: the model's `turn` index when that turn actually contains the span, else the unique turn containing the span verbatim, else the conversation's first turn. For a call the ref is the call record itself. Extractor version `geo-extract/v9d` (2026-10-04; v9c 2026-10-03; v8 before — all 347 stored rows are v8).
- **Agent-only chat threads are skipped** (the customer never wrote) — nothing to interpret, no LLM spend.
- **Not every word is a place, not every place a preference (2026-09-13 grading fixes).** Generic words («الموقع», «المكان», a «يا الشيخ» address) are not location mentions; a place named only for comparison or as the customer's origin («في الخبر أرخص») is `preference_role='none'`; a relative reference that restates already-recorded places («بين المناطق هذي») gets no separate record.
- **A side of a ROAD is a band, not a city zone (2026-09-15).** «غرب الملك فهد» is one `direction` anchor. The resolver reads the direction word, then tries the referent as a CITY (zone of its districts), then as a ROAD (a bounded `directional_band` on that side, default depth), and otherwise `needs_confirm('side_of_unknown_referent')`. Riyadh has both a King Fahd Road and a King Fahd District; only a line has sides, so a district is never guessed as the referent of a side. A mention with a district AND a side («العليا (غرب الملك فهد)») compiles to TWO include clauses of the group (AND): the band, plus the district — never a district id inside the band's road list. Element search passes the city in ENGLISH (the RPC filters `geo_elements.city='Riyadh'`) and also queries the «طريق …» form so the road outranks hospitals/metro stops that share the name.
- **Districts clipped to a side of a road = a custom shape (2026-09-15, second pass).** The operator's read of the first calib-003 map: «he wanted those districts but west of the road, not the entire length of the road». So a road side stated with districts is a QUALIFIER, not a separate area: `distributeRoadSide` (orchestrator) turns every include district ref in the expression into `district_side_clip` (district ids + the road, `side`) and removes the standalone band — including across groups, because the compiler puts each independent mention in its own OR group. At proposal time `hydrateClipGeometry` calls `wassell_districts_side_of_road(uuid[], road, side)` (PostGIS: split each district by the road, keep the pieces whose centroid is on that side; a district entirely on the other side is dropped and reported) and stores `clip_geojson` + per-district `clip_parts` NEXT TO the recipe (never instead of it). The review apply path turns the shape into one `drawn_area` location item per polygon (closed ring, `[lng, lat]`), falling back to districts + a side rule when no shape exists; the grader draws the shape and the card reads «حدّد: العليا (3.89 من 10.84 كم²)، المحمدية … — غرب طريق الملك فهد». Excludes are never distributed; two different road sides in one expression are left alone; diagonal sides are refused by the RPC.
- **Road sides, «near a place» and «between two roads» actually resolve (2026-10-03).** *Partly superseded on 2026-10-04 — see the next bullet, which is the current rule set: the connector folding in (a) («من» / «على» / «ل» between a direction and its road), the conjunction rule of (a) and of the third round's (2), the royal "undo a model rewrite" of (4) and the "two named cities make the road ask" clause of (5) were REPLACED; the rest still describes the code.* Measured in production before the fix: 0 of 11 `directional_band`, 0 of 10 `within_radius` and 0 of 5 `corridor` refs had ever resolved, because the resolver's operation companions (`radius_m`, `proximity`, `corridor`, `corridor_roads`) were never set outside tests — every road ended `corridor_underspecified`, every landmark `missing_radius`; a bare «جنوب» beside a separate road anchor resolved to the south half of the CITY; «شمال سلمان» resolved to a district called «سلمان»; and «قريب من الرياض بارك», extracted as city «الرياض», drew the whole city. Now `prepareEvidence` (`anchorPrep.ts`) prepares each mention's anchors before resolution — inside `runReviewFirst`, after the company rules, so the extract path, the review-only path, the backfill and the call audit all get it; the stored evidence rows are untouched (a re-review applies it). **Every decision is made from the TEXT of `mention_span`, by whole words — never by the order of the anchors and never by substring** (a review of the first version found a direction folded into an unrelated road elsewhere in the sentence, and «قرب» matched inside the district «العقربية»). Rules: (a) a bare direction folds with a road ONLY when the road's words follow the direction's in the text with nothing between but «من» / «على» / «ل»; the result is ONE direction anchor «<direction> <road>», which the existing road-side path turns into a `directional_band` — and, with a district in the mention, the existing merge turns into a `district_side_clip`. A city right after the direction owns it («شمال الرياض على طريق الملك فهد» is north Riyadh; the road stays its own anchor, which still asks), a «قريب من» road is never folded, an anchor that cannot be found in the text is never folded, and with several directions each pairs only with its own adjacent road; a bare direction joined to a road side by «او» / «و» («شرق او غرب الملك فهد») cannot be told from the city's side, so it asks (`side_of_unknown_referent`) instead of drawing the city's east; (b) a bare royal name («سلمان», «فهد», «خالد», «عبدالله», «عبدالعزيز», «فيصل», «سعود» — WITHOUT the article: «الفهد» is a real district in Najran, Bisha, Sharurah…, «السلمان» in Tabuk) right after a direction is the road «الملك <name>», never a district (the Riyadh district is always «حي الملك سلمان»); inside ONE direction anchor («جنوب سلمان») the referent is rewritten to «الملك سلمان»; (c) a stated `distance_m` belongs ONLY to the anchor that carries it (a folded road side keeps its own part's; a one-anchor road side uses its own as the band depth) — a venue's 2 km never becomes a road band's depth; «near X» is per ANCHOR: its role is `proximity`, or one of «قريب من / قريبة من / قرب / بالقرب من / جنب / بجنب / بجانب / جوار / بجوار / مشي من / near / close to / next to» stands as whole words right before it («تقريبا», «اقرب», «العقربية», «قربان», «مشيرفة» never count); (d) «بين طريقين» is NOT prepared — there is no "between two roads" geometry downstream, so both roads stay `needs_confirm('corridor_underspecified')` (two silent 3 km bands would break HARD RULE 4; `review.ts` also saves nothing for a corridor without a stated width); (e) a city is a «near X» target only when IT is the target (its role, the phrase right before it, or its own distance): followed by up to three more words before «و / او / في / بس / مع / اللي» or punctuation («قريب من الرياض بارك») it becomes a landmark with the full name, otherwise `needs_confirm('proximity_to_city')`. Any other CITY (not a region — see the third round below) in a mention with a road side / road / landmark is that element's SCOPE: its name is passed as the lookup `city`, so «بجدة شمال طريق الملك عبدالله» searches JEDDAH's road, and a road the named city does not have asks — it never falls back to the established city's namesake. A referent known to be a road (a folded road, a royal name, or a text starting «طريق / شارع / الطريق / الدائري») never tries a city zone: «شرق طريق الدمام» is not east Dammam. The prepared anchors are what resolve AND what compile/merge (the merge pairs resolutions with anchors by count), and they are the recipe's `source_anchors` — the road side first, so a chip is never labelled with the city. The merge drops a scope city / region from its admin side (it was about to become a «district» in a clip), and a mention whose anchors resolve to TWO DIFFERENT element rules (a road side AND a venue radius) stays unresolved with `unresolved_reference` instead of silently keeping the first. A named venue gathers POINT and POLYGON exact matches together (≈ 3,000 active polygons — malls, parks, universities, hospitals — could never be picked before): matches within 2 km of each other are ONE place (its polygon wins → `within_distance` of the shape; a lone point → `within_radius`), two distinct places → `needs_confirm('ambiguous_entity')` («جامعة الملك سعود» is both the campus and a metro station 11.7 km away). A `directional_band` now records its `side` from the parsed direction word, and `review.ts` saves that side — the old substring scan read «جنوب الدائري الشمالي» as NORTH; a legacy band without a side reads only the LEADING direction word. A `district_side_clip` whose computed clip keeps nothing saves NOTHING (it used to save the whole district it had just dropped), and a legacy clip with no shape saves nothing either; the card marks both untickable with «لا يقع جزء من الحي على هذا الجانب» / «تعذّر حساب الجزء». Each is audited SEQUENTIALLY by `auditCall` (`api/_lib/clientPrefs/callAudit.ts`): `call_audit_claim` (one winner; a failed or lease-expired row is re-claimed while attempts < 3) → `gatherCallConversation` (the same diarization builder the backfill uses) → no transcript ⇒ `skipped/no_transcript`, `speaker_labels='none'` ⇒ `skipped/unlabelled` (we can't tell who said what) → `extractPreferences` channel `call` → districts dropped → the client's current values (gone ⇒ `skipped/client_missing`) → `emptyOnlySuggestions` keeps only EMPTY fields → none ⇒ `done` (reason `no_preferences` / `all_filled`) → else one pending proposal (`chat_wid='call:<id>'`, `source='call'`, `call_at` = hang-up, `trigger='call_audit'`) → `call_audit_finish` (always, even after a failure — the error is recorded; a killed process leaves a lease that expires). Measured dry run on 60 days (2026-09-29): 26 real calls, 18 linked, 14 usable → 16 missed fields, 10 same, 9 differs (never proposed). The save (`/api/client-prefs/review`, `buildFillEmptyPatch`) re-checks every ticked field on the FRESH client row inside the versioned write and skips any that is no longer empty (`skipped_filled`).
- **Places from calls — the call audit's geography pass (2026-09-29, `2026-09-29_02_call_audit_geo.sql`).** Operator request "do places from calls", with the same never-overwrite posture. After the preference pass (same claim, same gathered conversation), `auditCall` runs the geography pass: unlabelled / no transcript / client gone ⇒ skipped; the client already has places (`parseLocationItems(location_items)` non-empty) ⇒ `skipped/has_places`; else `analyzeChatConversation(sb, clientId, callId, { conversation })` — the chat card's pipeline, NOT a fork: no checkpoint ⇒ extract; a checkpoint (the 45 calibration call checkpoints) with no newer message or graded evidence ⇒ review-only, no LLM extraction, graded rows never deleted. `AnalyzeOutcome.minted_proposal_id` is the proposal THIS run made (null ⇒ `done/no_places`); it is recorded as `call_pref_audit.geo_proposal_id` by `call_audit_geo_finish`. **Company rule 3 (calls only):** `applyCallSpeakerGuard` (companyRules.ts) demotes to `preference_role='none'` every non-agent mention whose `mention_span` (folded like the preference quotes, `normalizeForQuote`) is not in a `speaker==='client'` turn — applied by `evidenceForReview` (chatCard.ts) before `runReviewFirst` in BOTH the extract and the review-only path, so re-reviews over stored evidence are guarded too; chats are unchanged. Calls audited before this existed (pref status `done`) get a GEO-ONLY pass (`needs_prefs=false`, `call_audit_geo_claim`) that never re-runs the preference extractor. **Save:** `/api/geo-preference/review` asks `isCallAuditProposal` (a `call_pref_audit.geo_proposal_id` match); for those, `applyToClient` refuses when the client has places — checked on the first read AND on the fresh row inside `recordSaveWithRetry` (`buildGeoApplyData` throws `ReviewError(409, 'client_has_places')`), before the proposal is touched, so it stays pending for the rep to dismiss. Chat proposals keep the union exactly. **Card:** `loadPrefsCard` returns `call_geo` — audit-minted proposals only (pending / must_confirm, or decided in the last 24 h), each with `loadChatCard(call id)` and the client's FRESH `has_places`; an open one with no drawable line is not shown.
- **Extractor prompt v3 (2026-09-29).** Both channels: a customer looking to RENT («إيجار», «استئجار», «تأجير») gets no `purchase_objective` and no `budget` (a rent is not a purchase budget) — the dry run recorded «عم ندور الحين على تأجير» as residential + budget. Call channel: what the salesperson says (a summary «أنت تبحث عن شقة…», a suggestion, a restatement of the lead form) is not the customer's preference unless the customer confirms it in their OWN line, and the quote must come from a customer line.
- **Transcribed voice notes are customer text (2026-09-27).** An inbound `kind='audio'` message with a `chat_messages.transcript` (filled by the inbound-media worker) is rendered as the customer line «(رسالة صوتية) <transcript>» in the gathered conversation, counts toward `customer_messages` / staleness, and toward "unread". A voice note with no usable transcript (status none/failed, or media lost) shows as an amber mic badge on the card instead.
- **Review-first, always.** The orchestrator's only side effect is one `pending` row in `geo_pref_proposals` per conversation; `auto_write_enabled` is forced off in the backfill's run context regardless of the DB flag.
- **Idempotent per conversation.** Re-running a client replaces the prior model rows for each of its conversations (by real conversation id); evidence ids are re-minted, so a calibration batch built on old ids must be rebuilt.
- **Grader is admin-only** and shows, per card, the channel («مكالمة هاتفية» / «محادثة واتساب»), the customer's first name, the ONE transcript the mention came from with the span highlighted, the phrase, and the AI's plain-Arabic reading. Keyboard 1/2/3.
- **Conversation grader (`?view=chat`, added 2026-09-13).** One screen per conversation: the chat as bubbles (or the call transcript), every mention with its reading and a verdict, then a **map of what the AI actually selected** — the proposal's location items drawn from the real district boundaries (`wassell_district_shapes_by_ids`) and compiled element rules (`wassell_preview_geo_items`); include = copper, exclude = red — with a map verdict per conversation saved as a `geo_pref_labels` row (`subject_kind='checkpoint'`, `field='map.verdict'`). The map reads like a normal map: the Wassel basemap with Google's district and road names ON (the picker's label-suppressed style is NOT used here), a name pill on every drawn shape (colliding pills nudged apart), the view fitted to the WANTED shapes only (an exclude band along a 40 km road must not zoom the map out), excludes drawn light and underneath. Each mention also says what it became on the map («حدّد: النرجس (الرياض)», «لم يُحدَّد حي حقيقي — يحتاج تأكيدًا», or «ليس تفضيلًا»).
- **The map is real now (fixed 2026-09-13).** Until then the compiler wrote the district *names* into the expression (`geo_data_version='stub'`) and the resolver's real output only fed the gate signals — every proposal carried names, never district ids, so nothing was ever selected on the map. `mergeResolutionsIntoPreference` (orchestrator) now overwrites each mention's stub recipe with the resolver recipe (district / element ids) when every anchor of that mention resolved; a partially resolved mention keeps the stub so `geo_data_version !== 'stub'` always means real ids. Two prerequisites landed with it: the runner reviews on the **persisted** evidence ids (so `geo:<id>` refs and `source_evidence_ids` join to `geo_pref_evidence`), and the resolver gets an **established city** — the client's single `preferred_city`, else Riyadh as the organizational default — because bare names are namesakes across the Kingdom (النرجس ×6 cities, الياسمين ×20) and the resolver rightly refuses to guess without a city.

## User flows
1. **Backfill (admin):** `POST /api/geo-preference/backfill {action:'enqueue', clientIds}` → `{action:'process'}` in bounded batches → per client: gather conversations → per conversation: extract → persist evidence + one checkpoint → review-first → pending proposal. Progress via `GET ?runId`.
2. **Grade (admin):** open `/geo-grade?batch=<id>` → cards ordered by client then time → Right / Wrong / Not sure saves one `overall.verdict` label per evidence row → "All done" screen; verdicts are editable by re-visiting a card.
3. **Review (rep):** open proposals listed by `/api/geo-preference/proposals`; confirm / reject / edit via `/api/geo-preference/review` (audit row per action). A confirm/edit IS the apply — the human tap is the authorization; `auto_write_enabled` only governs the (off) automatic path.
4. **Confirm in the chat (rep, 2026-09-27):** open a client-linked WhatsApp chat → the «تفضيلات العميل» card (usually already read by the cron, or read on open when the customer wrote something unread; «اقرأ المواقع» / «أعد القراءة» force a read, up to a minute) → untick any wrong line → «احفظ في ملف العميل» (writes the ticked lines to the client's `location_items`, merged, never wiping what is there) or «تجاهل». Later, «حدّث» re-reads the chat after the customer writes again.
5. **Empty states:** a client with no calls and no client-written chat completes with no proposal; a card whose transcript is missing shows «لا يوجد نص محادثة محفوظ لهذا العميل».

## Open questions / known limitations
- **The 2026-10-03 / 2026-10-04 road-side, landmark and check work is NOT re-graded yet.** It changes what reps see on the card for any mention with a road, a venue, a distance or a bare direction; the calibration batch should be re-graded (review-only). Existing stored evidence (v8) has no `distance_m`, so a re-review of it still asks for the distance; the folding, the scope, the checks and the holds DO apply on a re-review.
- **Accepted asks (2026-10-04 — by design these ask instead of drawing):** any region beside another place («شمال طريق الملك فهد بالمنطقة الشرقية», «شمال القصيم»); a bare direction beside a road side, a road or a venue («الشمال غرب طريق الملك فهد», «ابي بالشمال وغرب الملك فهد»); a bare direction with a named city that is not the client's, unless the city is directly after it («في جدة بالشمال» from a Riyadh client; «شمال جدة» resolves); «شمال او شرق جدة»; two named cities plus another place («شمال الرياض او شمال جدة» included); a side that does not run along its road («شرق طريق الدمام», «شمال طريق الملك فهد», «غرب طريق مكة»); two-campus venues and chain malls («جامعة الأميرة نورة», «جامعة الملك سعود», «العثيم مول»); a stated distance that is not in the customer's words (or is said only in another message); a bare direction, an unscoped road / venue, or a namesake district when another active mention names another city. The never-wrong corpus also records one shape that asks where a place was expected: the glued «الروضة شمال جدة» / «الروابي شمال الدمام» (district + ONE direction anchor) when another city is named, because I9 forbids assuming the client's city for the bare district (`established_city_contradicted`); the split form resolves.
- **Residual wrong-place paths no check can see (documented, not fixed):** (a) a bare royal name after a direction where the customer meant a district — it still needs an exact King … Road in the scope city and a valid axis; (b) a city word followed by an uncatalogued venue with no proximity phrase (I8 covers catalogued names only); (c) a client with no city on record falls back to «الرياض» when nothing in the conversation names a city; (d) «غرب طريق الملك سلمان» as a STANDALONE band passes the whole-road check (0.507) although the side is weak — a clip uses the local measure, which catches it; (e) the venue search returns at most 30 candidates per query, so a far namesake ranked lower could be missed.
- **The 0.40 / 0.60 axis thresholds are a judgement**, set on the measured shares of 14 Riyadh roads; re-check them against the re-grade. A road with no geometry, or not found by `wassell_geo_road_axis`, always asks.
- **Structured extraction (`geo-extract/v10`, `structure.ts` — the model states "north OF what", "near WHAT", "how far in the customer's words") is designed but NOT built.** It ships only after the checks above pass their release gates and a live compliance count of ≥ 90%.
- **«بين طريقين» still asks.** There is no "between two roads" geometry downstream; until there is, a corridor stays `corridor_underspecified` (the resolver's corridor branch is only reachable from tests).
- **Re-backfill of the 23 `calib-001` clients has NOT been run yet.** Old rows (`conversation_id` = client uuid) must be purged first and the batch rebuilt — runbook §2a.
- **Calibration grading 2026-09-13 (batch `calib-001` after the per-channel re-extraction, one grader):** 90 right / 13 wrong / 0 unsure (87%; calls 83/94, chats 7/9). 6 of the 13 were speaker attribution on unlabelled calls (the rep's scripted opener «لك رغبة بشراء شقة أو دور بشمال الرياض» read as the customer's wish; القيروان; the reverse once) — that is what the Hatif-diarization change above targets; 3 were non-place words, 1 a comparison city, 1 a redundant relative reference, 2 (`التعاون`/`الازدهار`, a customer question) are still under discussion. The diarized path has NOT been re-graded yet.
- Inbound calls have no channel rule (too few channel-labelled inbound calls on prod to measure), so an inbound call whose agent never introduces the company keeps `speaker='unknown'` turns.
- A short span that appears in several turns («الرياض») with no usable `turn` hint falls back to conversation-level provenance (first turn's ref).
- Re-running a client re-mints its checkpoint, and the old pending proposal's `checkpoint_id` is nulled by the FK — the proposal-store dedup then cannot match it, so purge or supersede old pending proposals before a re-run. (The chat card's analyze does this itself.)
- **A plain `confirm` through `/api/geo-preference/review` writes unresolved lines as junk (found 2026-09-27, NOT fixed there).** `geoPreferenceToLocationItems` turns a `geo_data_version='stub'` recipe — whose ids are bare names — into location items whose element id is an Arabic word; the grader hides only non-uuid DISTRICT items. The chat card is safe (it always prunes unresolved lines and sends `edit`); any other surface that confirms a proposal as-is is not.
- **A chat is read from its NEWEST 120 messages** (`MAX_MESSAGES_PER_CHAT`; fixed 2026-09-27 — until then it read the OLDEST 120, so a long chat's latest messages were never seen). What the customer said before those 120 is not re-read.
- **Graded conversations cannot pick up new messages** — the rerun is review-only by design, so the card says so instead of offering «حدّث».
- **Concurrent reads of one chat are serialised by the `chat_read_state` lease (2026-09-27)** — a second reader gets `not_claimed`. A crashed reader's lease expires after 300 s.
- **The call audit reads each call once.** A call is never re-audited after `done` / `skipped` — if the extractor improves, old calls are not re-read. A failed attempt is retried at most 3 times (then it stays `failed` in `call_pref_audit`). Calls whose transcript has no speaker labels are skipped, not guessed. Places ARE taken from calls since 2026-09-29, but only for a client with no places — a client who already has places never gets call places, even when the call names new ones (that is the never-overwrite rule, by design). The geography pass is retried at most 3 times; a call whose preference audit is exhausted-failed never gets a geo pass.
- **The chat auto-read's SQL candidate scan only looks back 7 days**, and a chat that failed 8 reads in a row is left to the rep (an open / «أعد القراءة» read that succeeds resets the count). A voice note whose media was lost never flips its `transcript_status` off `pending` (the worker's terminal path does not write it), so it holds a read back for at most 10 minutes.
